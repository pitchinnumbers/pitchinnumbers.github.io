Every match, three steps

How it works

01 / Before kick-off

Preview

Both teams read like an opposition report: form, xG for and against, where their shots come from, who decides games — and two or three predictions I can be held to.

02 / After the final whistle

Post-match report

An xG timeline, both shot maps and three points: what worked, what didn't, and what should change.

03 / The check

Did the data get it right?

Every prediction is graded in public. When I'm wrong, I explain why — that's where the learning is.
