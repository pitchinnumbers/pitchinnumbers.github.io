Match of the Week

One match, one analytical cycle — the same way a club's data department prepares for and reviews a game.

01 · BEFORE

Preview

Opposition-style report on both teams: form, xG for and against, where shots come from, key players — plus 2–3 concrete predictions.

02 · AFTER

Post-match report

xG timeline, shot maps and three takeaways: what worked, what didn't, what should change.

03 · ACCOUNTABILITY

Did the data get it right?

Every prediction is graded in public. When I'm wrong, I explain why — that's where the learning is.
