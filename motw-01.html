Liverpool 4-0 Man United

Everyone will say it was the red card. The real story is the last 30 minutes.

United down to 10 men from the 8th minute. Pochettino's first game in charge.

01 / Turning point

82 minutes of 11 v 10

Yoro was sent off for a foul on Isak. Szoboszlai scored the free kick, Wirtz volleyed in Ngumoha's cross three minutes later: 2-0 by the 13th minute. After the red card, Liverpool built 3.98 xG to United's 0.59.

02 / The insight

Cruise, then the kill

At 2-0 Liverpool took their foot off: 0.12 xG between minutes 45 and 60. Then the last half hour produced 2.66 xG, 61% of their total, with the game already won. That's a team hunting goals, not managing a lead.

03 / Evidence

Liverpool lived in the box

19 of Liverpool's 28 shots came from inside the box. United had 4 shots in theirs all game. Bubble size = xG, peach = goal.

04 / Player spotlight

The 18-year-old

Academy graduate Rio Ngumoha crossed for Wirtz's volley, then finished Barcola's run for 3-0. Alexander Isak won the foul that got Yoro sent off, but didn't score.

My take

The red card decided the scale of this: 82 minutes against 10 men will do that. But 2.66 xG in the last half hour is a team that kept pushing. And United's problem wasn't only the red card: they had 4 shots in the box all game.
