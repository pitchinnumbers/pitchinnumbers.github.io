Liverpool 4-0 Man United: the real story is the last 30 minutes

Everyone will say it was the red card. It decided how big this got. But the data shows Liverpool cruising at 2-0, then going for the kill.

01 / Context

How it unfolded

Leny Yoro was sent off in the 8th minute for a foul on Isak. Szoboszlai scored the free kick, and three minutes later Wirtz volleyed in Ngumoha's cross. 2-0 by the 13th minute, in Pochettino's first game in charge of United.

02 / Turning point

82 minutes of 11 v 10

After the red card Liverpool built 3.98 xG to United's 0.59. Expected with a man extra, but the shape of the line matters more than its height.

03 / The insight

Cruise, then the kill

At 2-0 Liverpool took their foot off: just 0.12 xG between minutes 45 and 60. Then the last half hour produced 2.66 xG, 61% of their total, with the game already won.

04 / Evidence

Liverpool lived in the box

19 of Liverpool's 28 shots came from inside the box. United had 4 shots in theirs all game.

05 / Player spotlight

The 18-year-old

Rio Ngumoha: a goal, an assist, 4 shots worth 0.71 xG. Alexander Isak had 6 shots worth 1.31 xG and didn't score, but he won the foul that got Yoro sent off.

06 / My take

Was it just the red card?

The red card decided the scale of this: 82 minutes against 10 men will do that. But 2.66 xG in the last half hour, when the game was already won, is a team hunting goals, not managing a lead. And United's problem wasn't only the red card: they had 4 shots in the box all game.
